Flywheel

A calm habit tracker for Android and iOS.

Check off daily habits, hit a number each day or week, and count clear days for the things you're avoiding. Freeze days keep a streak alive when life gets in the way.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Flywheel has no account and no cloud sync, so your habits don't move to a new phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

How do I restore my ad-free plan on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with, open Go ad-free in the app, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from: Google Play subscriptions or App Store subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. If you cancel, ads stay off until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: request one through Google Play or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My reminders don't show up

Check that notifications are allowed for Flywheel in your phone's settings. On Android, some phones also stop reminders when battery optimization is on for the app; setting Flywheel's battery use to Unrestricted fixes this.

What are freeze days?

Each month a habit gets a few freeze days (you choose how many). If you miss a day, or slip on something you're avoiding, a freeze covers it and your streak keeps going. Freezes are only used when there's a streak to protect.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, you'll find Ad privacy choices at the bottom of the Progress screen. You can also limit personalized ads in your phone's settings. See the Privacy Policy.
