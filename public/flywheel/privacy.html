Flywheel

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Flywheel is a habit tracker made by Ali Khamesy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Flywheel app on Android or iOS.

The short version

  • Your habits, logs and reminders stay on your phone. We don't run servers or accounts, and we never receive them.
  • If you haven't gone ad-free, the app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device information to show and measure ads.
  • If you buy an ad-free plan, Google Play or the App Store handles the payment, and RevenueCat confirms the purchase for us. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your information, and the app has no analytics or tracking of its own.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter in Flywheel is stored only on your phone: habit names and details, the days you log, slips, freeze days, reminder times and settings. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account, sync or cloud backup. Deleting a habit removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

The app also keeps a few things on your device to work properly, such as whether you have an ad-free plan and which celebration screens you've already seen.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no message is sent from a server.
  • Photos: only to save a share card image when you choose to save one. The app doesn't read or upload your photos.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have an ad-free plan, Flywheel shows a banner ad and, at most once a day, a full-screen ad after you finish the day's habits. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID or, on iOS, your advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Device and app information, such as the device model, operating system and language
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice anytime under Ad privacy choices on the Progress screen. Everywhere, you can limit personalized ads in your device's privacy settings: the Ads setting on Android (where it is depends on your phone), or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS.

With an ad-free plan, the ads SDK isn't started at all, so no ad requests are made. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play, App Store and RevenueCat)

Ad-free plans are bought through Google Play or the App Store, which process the payment under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, email address or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and keep your ad-free status up to date. RevenueCat receives your purchase and transaction records, a random ID the app creates (not linked to your name), and basic device and app information such as platform, OS version, app version, country and IP address. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • The app has no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of its own.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

Flywheel is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used a purchase or ad feature in a way that concerns you, contact us.

Keeping and deleting information

  • On your device: kept until you delete the habit or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: kept by the store and RevenueCat as long as needed to confirm your purchases and meet legal obligations. Contact us and we'll ask RevenueCat to delete its record for your app ID.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under its own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under California law, the GDPR in Europe or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it's used. Since your habit data never leaves your device, most requests concern the purchase records described above. Contact us and we'll help, usually within 30 days.

Google and RevenueCat may process information in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards described in their policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app or its store listing.
